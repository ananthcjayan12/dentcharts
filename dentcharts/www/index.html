About Us

Trusted specialists focused on confident, healthy smiles

Dr. Jacobs Orthodontic Centre & Advanced Dental Care brings together specialist orthodontics, general dentistry, and patient-first service under one roof.

About Prof. Dr. Jacob John

Prof. Dr. Jacob John is an internationally recognized Consultant Specialist Orthodontist with over 20 years of experience across Qatar, Oman, the United Kingdom, Ireland, and India.

He completed specialist orthodontic training and fellowships from the Royal College of Surgeons in Ireland, Royal College of Surgeons of Edinburgh, and the Royal College of Physicians and Surgeons of Glasgow. He has served as Specialist Orthodontist at Aster Medical Centre, Qatar, and as Professor and Postgraduate Guide in Orthodontics at Dr. Moopen’s Medical College, Wayanad.

Dr. Jacob specializes in Invisalign aligners, self-ligating braces, lingual orthodontics, and growth modulation therapies to solve complex orthodontic concerns.

About Dr. Maria K. Pullazhi

Dr. Maria K. Pullazhi is a Chief Dental Surgeon with over 11 years of clinical experience in general dentistry, holding both a BDS and an MBA in Hospital Administration.

She is known for accurate diagnosis, effective treatment planning, and clear communication that helps patients make informed decisions about their oral health.

Dr. Maria helps maintain high standards of patient care, safety, and ethical dental practice while creating a comfortable and welcoming environment for every patient.
